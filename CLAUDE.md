## Tooling
Leverage `mkosi` wherever you can:
- Build the image: `mkosi build`
- Test in VM (SAFE - does not touch your disk!): `mkosi qemu`
- when in doubt, read `mkosi documentation` so you can see the full capabilities, including other tools!
- also dont be afraid to use `man` to get the most information

We are going to be repartitioning a real disk, so be careful and make sure to double-check your work

be sure to emphasize systemd technologies as much as possble
